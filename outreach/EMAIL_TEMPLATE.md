### Sending tips

1. **Verify emails** on official websites before bulk send; parliament addresses follow `firstname.surname.mp@parliament.go.ke` but should be confirmed on [parliament.go.ke](https://www.parliament.go.ke).
2. Send **10–20 emails per day** to reduce spam filters; personalize the first line.
3. Do **not** publish personal member phone numbers from the payment sheet in external mailings without consent.
4. For State House / DP, use **formal tone**, PDF attachment, and physical copy to the P.O. Box if no reply in 21 days.
