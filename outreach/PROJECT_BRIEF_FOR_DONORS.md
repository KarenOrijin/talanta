## Why this is a strong case for support

1. **Grassroots momentum:** Nearly 30% of the goal is already raised from members across **129 active groups**—not a top-down ask only.
2. **Transparent tracking:** Weekly payments are recorded **per member**, with running balances and group totals (your Excel dashboard). Donors can see exactly who gave and what remains.
3. **Accountability:** Church accounts (`accounts@pefachurch.org`), county/NGCDF channels, and optional third-party review (e.g. NCCK, TI Kenya) can be cited for external givers.
4. **Local impact:** Reliable transport helps elders, youth, and outreach teams in **Webuye and Western Kenya**, where rural mobility is a real barrier.

## What you are asking for

- **Financial partnership** toward the remaining **~KSh 3.09M** (or partial sponsorship of the coach).
- **In-kind support:** vehicle sourcing, insurance, NTSA compliance, or maintenance workshop partnership.
- **Visibility / advocacy:** signal from leaders (county, NG-CDF, national church) that the project is legitimate and tracked.
