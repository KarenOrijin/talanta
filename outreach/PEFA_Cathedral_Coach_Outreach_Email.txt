Subject: Request for Support: PEFA Cathedral Webuye Coach Project

Your Excellency / Honourable [Name],

Warm greetings from PEFA Cathedral, Webuye, Bungoma County.

We write on behalf of Bishop Frank Erima and the Coach Project Committee to tell you about a project our congregation has taken on, and to humbly ask you to stand with us.

OUR STORY
Ten years ago, PEFA Cathedral Webuye began in a small mud house. We had no sponsor and no outside funding, only a congregation that believed God had a bigger plan for us. Every Sunday, members gave what they could. Sunday by Sunday, gift by gift, that faithfulness built the church we worship in today.

Now we are ready for the next step. Having built a house of worship, we want to take the church out to the people. A coach will let us reach the community around us and serve Webuye beyond our four walls.

THE PROJECT
Our church is buying a coach to carry our congregation, choir, youth and mission teams. It will take members to services and church events, and serve the wider Webuye community. The coach will cost KSh 8,000,000.

WHAT OUR MEMBERS HAVE ALREADY DONE
Just as we did when we built our church, we did not wait for outside help to begin. Since May 2026, 182 of our members have organised themselves into 14 contribution groups and pledged KSh 4.3 million among themselves. So far, 133 of them have given KSh 1,309,820, Sunday after Sunday, from their own pockets. We are now asking for partners to help us finish what our members have started.

HOW YOUR CONTRIBUTION WILL BE ACCOUNTED FOR
I developed the church's digital payment tracker for this project. Every shilling is recorded each Sunday, contributor by contributor, and the totals update automatically. The tracker is open to the committee for checking at any time. In addition:
• Every contribution receives an official church receipt.
• We will send you a progress report, and a final report once the coach is bought, showing exactly where the money went.
• The project is overseen by named, accountable officials: Bishop Frank Erima; Mr Pius Wanyama (Chairman); Mr Fred Machasio (Vice Chairman); Mama Jenniffer Wanyonyi (Treasurer); and Mrs Gladys N. Wanyama (Secretary).

YOUR SUPPORT WILL NOT BE FORGOTTEN
The names of our partners will be recorded on the church's honour roll and acknowledged publicly at the dedication service when the coach is handed over. We would be honoured to have you with us on that day.

OUR REQUEST
We humbly ask you to consider:
1. Making a contribution towards the coach, and/or
2. Gracing one of our fundraising services as our Guest of Honour.

You are also most welcome to visit us at PEFA Cathedral Webuye at any time, to worship with us, meet the committee and see the project for yourself.
