# HNW Christian philanthropist leads (400 rows)

## Important (please read before emailing)

Most wealthy Christians **do not publish personal email addresses**. Spam, security, and family-office practice mean outreach almost always goes through:

| `contact_type` | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **personal_public** | Email published on the person’s or family ministry site (safe to try first) |
| **foundation** | Family foundation or donor-advised fund (preferred for large gifts) |
| **ministry_office** | Public inbox for the leader’s ministry (gate to executive team) |
| **corporate_csr** | Company philanthropy or customer service route |

In Excel, **filter `contact_type` = personal_public** for direct family inboxes (only a handful are truly personal).

For billionaires and celebrities, **never guess @gmail addresses**. Use `foundation` or `ministry_office` and ask for the partnerships desk.
