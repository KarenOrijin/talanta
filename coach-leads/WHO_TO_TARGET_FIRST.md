## How to work the 1,000-row list

1. Open `GLOBAL_PHILANTHROPY_LEADS_1000.xlsx`
2. Filter **outreach_wave** = 1, then 2, then 3…
3. Sort by **priority** (Critical → High → Medium)
4. Filter **funding_fit** = High for church-coach fit
5. For **verify_email = yes**, confirm on the **source_url** before send
6. Personalize the first line: their office, constituency, or foundation mission
